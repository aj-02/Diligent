Logic (column -> derivation)

MATERIAL GROUP 2	Pass VBRP-MATNR to MVKE-MATNR for MVGR3 is not equal to blank and VKORG=4000 and take MVGR3. pass MVKE-MVGR3 to TVM3T-MVGR3 and take BEZEI
MATERIAL GROUP 5	Pass VBRP-MATNR to MVKE-MATNR for MVGR4 is not equal to blank and VKORG=4000 and take MVGR4. pass MVKE-MVGR4 to TVM4T-MVG41 and take BEZEI

MATERIAL GROUP	For VBRP-MATNR show MATKL.
PACK SIZE	Pass ALV MATNR to MARM-MATNR where MARA-MEINH is not equal to ALV UOM and take MARM-UMREN. If the MEINH is equal to ML or GM, then divide the value with 1000.
Gross Weight	Pass ALV MATNR to MARA-MATNR and take MARA-BRGEW
